# Payments On-Call Handbook

## Rotation

The payments on-call rotation is one week long and hands over every Monday at 10:00 CET. The
secondary on-call is the previous week's primary.

## Response targets

A SEV1 page must be acknowledged within 5 minutes and a SEV2 page within 15 minutes. If the primary
does not acknowledge, the page escalates to the secondary and then to the engineering manager.

## Compensation for on-call

Engineers receive a fixed on-call allowance per week of rotation, plus time off in lieu for any
incident work between 22:00 and 07:00.
