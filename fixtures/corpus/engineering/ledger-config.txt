Ledger consumer configuration notes (platform team)

ledger.retry_backoff_ms controls the initial delay between ledger write retries. The default is 250
and the delay doubles on each attempt up to ledger.max_attempts, which defaults to 8.

ledger.batch_size controls how many ledger entries are written per transaction. The default is 100.
Raising it above 500 has caused lock contention during month-end close and is not recommended.

Changes to these settings are made in config/ledger.properties and require a restart of the ledger
consumer. They are reviewed by the payments team.
