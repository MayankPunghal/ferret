# Parental Leave Policy (Confidential)

## Entitlement

Employees with at least six months of service are entitled to 20 weeks of fully paid parental leave,
which can be taken in up to three blocks within two years of the birth or adoption.

## Return to work

Employees may return part-time at 60% for up to eight weeks after parental leave without a change to
their contract.
