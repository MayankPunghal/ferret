# Travel and Expense Policy (2024)

## Meals and per diem

When travelling for business, employees may claim a daily meal allowance (per diem) of EUR 45 for
domestic travel and EUR 60 for international travel.

## Hotels

Hotel bookings must be made through the travel portal. The nightly cap is EUR 150 outside capital
cities and EUR 220 in capital cities.

## Submitting expenses

Expenses must be submitted within 60 days with itemized receipts.
