# Software Subscription Refund Policy

## Refund window

Customers on monthly or annual software subscriptions may request a full refund within 14 days of
the initial purchase. After 14 days, annual subscriptions are refunded pro rata for unused full
months; monthly subscriptions are not refunded.

## How refunds are issued

Refunds are issued to the original payment method within 10 business days. Finance approves refunds
above EUR 5,000.
