# Travel and Expense Policy (2025)

This version replaces the 2024 policy from 1 April 2025.

## Meals and per diem

When travelling for business, employees may claim a daily meal allowance (per diem) of EUR 55 for
domestic travel and EUR 70 for international travel.

## Hotels

Hotel bookings must be made through the travel portal. The nightly cap is EUR 170 outside capital
cities and EUR 240 in capital cities.

## Submitting expenses

Expenses must be submitted within 30 days with itemized receipts. Late claims need manager approval.
